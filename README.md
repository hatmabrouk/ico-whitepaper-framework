# ICO White Paper Framework

## Overview

This repository contains the datasets, metadata, code, and research materials supporting the development of a harmonized Initial Coin Offering (ICO) white paper framework.

The project forms part of a Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) research study aimed at identifying common disclosure elements across highly funded ICO white papers and proposing a standardized framework to improve transparency, comparability, and information quality.

---

## Research Objectives

1. Compile a corpus of highly funded ICO white papers and associated metadata.
2. Identify common disclosure elements present across the corpus.
3. Compare identified elements with academic, regulatory, and industry best practices.
4. Develop a harmonized ICO white paper framework and minimum disclosure checklist.

---

## Research Methodology

The study follows a four-stage methodology:

### Stage 1: Corpus Development

A corpus of highly funded ICO white papers is compiled using data obtained from CryptoRank and supporting industry sources.

### Stage 2: Disclosure Element Identification

The corpus is analyzed to identify recurring disclosure elements and structural patterns across ICO white papers.

### Stage 3: Best Practice Comparison

The identified disclosure elements are compared against recommendations derived from academic literature, regulatory documents, industry guidance, and expert interviews.

### Stage 4: Framework Development

Findings are synthesized into a harmonized ICO white paper framework and minimum disclosure checklist.
